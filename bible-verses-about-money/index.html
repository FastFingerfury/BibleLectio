Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about money?

The Bible speaks to money in verses such as 1 Timothy 6:10, “For the love of money is the root of all evil”, and Hebrews 13:5, “Let your conversation be without covetousness” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about money are most loved?

Among the most loved is 1 Timothy 6:10, “For the love of money is the root of all evil.” Others are Hebrews 13:5, Ecclesiastes 5:10 and Proverbs 22:7.
