Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about faith and trust?

The Bible speaks to faith and trust in verses such as Proverbs 3:5-6, “Trust in the LORD with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding”, and Psalms 56:3, “What time I am afraid, I will trust in thee” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about faith and trust are most loved?

Among the most loved is Proverbs 3:5-6, “Trust in the LORD with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding.” Others are Psalms 56:3, Psalms 37:5 and Romans 10:17.
