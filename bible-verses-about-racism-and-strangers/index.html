Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about racism and strangers?

The Bible speaks to racism and strangers in verses such as Romans 2:11, “For there is no respect of persons with God”, and John 7:24, “Judge not according to the appearance, but judge righteous judgment” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about racism and strangers are most loved?

Among the most loved is Leviticus 19:33-34, “And if a stranger sojourn with thee in your land, ye shall not vex him.” Others are Romans 2:11, John 7:24 and Galatians 3:28.
