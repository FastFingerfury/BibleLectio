Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about rulers and laws?

The Bible speaks to rulers and laws in verses such as Romans 13:1, “Let every soul be subject unto the higher powers”, and Psalms 22:28, “For the kingdom is the LORD’S: and he is the governor among the nations” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about rulers and laws are most loved?

Among the most loved is Romans 13:1, “Let every soul be subject unto the higher powers.” Others are 1 Peter 2:13-14, 1 Timothy 2:1-2 and Psalms 22:28.
