The cross dial.

Press and hold any verse in BibleLectio and everything you can do with it opens under your finger: save Bible verses to your library, link and cross-link verses, bookmark and highlight them, share them and underline words. One move, one gesture, no menus.

Try it in BibleLectio
Add verse Link verse Colour Bookmark verse Send verse to Dictionary Pin verse Linked Share verse Copy verse Underline Many bookmarks

How it works.

  1. One gesture, multiple options.Press and hold for the cross dial, with four options to choose from.
  2. Slide for more.Slide to any of the four, or press it, and it splits into two more options.
Circle 1 of 4

Add or send

Save Bible verses and words to your library. Slide up to the brain.

Add to library

Saves the verse to your library, into the cortex you have open, and a brain appears before the verse. Pull the page from its handle at the screen’s edge to see it in your library, tap it to open it, and pull the page back to the Bible. Or tap the brain before the verse to go straight to it in your library.

Send verse

Choose which cortex in your library the verse goes to, or make a new cortex as you send it. To see it there, pull the page into your library, tap the half brain at the top right and tap that cortex.

Dictionary

Save the word under your finger to your Dictionary. Hold a few words and they become one new word of yours. Tap the word in the Bible and its card opens with what each dictionary says of it, such as Strong’s, Easton’s and Webster’s: tap one to read it.

light
Strong’sEaston’sWebster’s

phōs (φῶς): luminousness (in the widest application, natural or artificial, abstract or concrete, literal or figurative). KJV: fire, light. — Strong’s G5457

The offspring of the divine command (Gen. 1:3). “All the more joyous emotions of the mind, all the pleasing sensations of the frame, all the happy hours of domestic intercourse were habitually described among the Hebrews under imagery derived from light” (1 Kings 11:36; Isa. 58:8; Esther 8:16; Ps. 97:11). Light came also naturally to typify true religion and the felicity it imparts (Ps. 119:105; Isa. 8:20; Matt. 4:16, etc.), and the glorious inheritance of the redeemed (Col. 1:12; Rev. 21:23-25). God is said to dwell in light inaccessible (1 Tim. 6:16). It frequently signifies instruction (Matt. 5:16; John 5:35). In its highest sense it is applied to Christ as the “Sun of righteousness” (Mal. 4:2; Luke 2:32; John 1:7-9). God is styled “the Father of lights” (James 1:17). It is used of angels (2 Cor. 11:14), and of John the Baptist, who was a “burning and a shining light” (John 5:35), and of all true disciples, who are styled “the light of the world” (Matt. 5:14). — Easton’s 1897

That agent, force, or action in nature by the operation of which upon the organs of sight, objects are rendered visible or luminous. — Webster’s 1913

Questions about the cross dial.

What is the cross dial in BibleLectio?

The cross dial is how you do everything with a Bible verse in BibleLectio. Press and hold any verse and four circles open under your finger: save it to your library, link and cross-link verses, colour and share them, bookmark and underline. Slide to a circle and lift, and it is done, with no menus.

How do I bookmark a verse in BibleLectio?

Hold the verse, slide down to the bookmark and lift. The verse glows so you find it again, and once you sign in your bookmarks are on every device.

How do I link two Bible verses together?

In BibleLectio, hold a verse, slide left to the link and lift on Link verse. Then tap any other verse, in any book, and the two are joined. Every verse joined to it is listed under it.

How do I save a Bible verse into a cortex?

Hold the verse in BibleLectio and slide up to the brain. Add to library puts it in the cortex you have open, and Send verse lets you choose another cortex or make a new one.

Can I colour a word everywhere it appears in the Bible?

Yes. In BibleLectio, hold the word, slide right to Colour and pick a colour. It can colour that word in the whole Bible or only in this verse.
