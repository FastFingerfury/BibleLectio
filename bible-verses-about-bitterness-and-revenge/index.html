Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about bitterness and revenge?

The Bible speaks to bitterness and revenge in verses such as Leviticus 19:18, “Thou shalt not avenge, nor bear any grudge against the children of thy people, but thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself”, and Romans 12:19, “Dearly beloved, avenge not yourselves, but rather give place unto wrath” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about bitterness and revenge are most loved?

Among the most loved is Leviticus 19:18, “Thou shalt not avenge, nor bear any grudge against the children of thy people, but thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.” Others are Romans 12:19, Ephesians 4:31-32 and Hebrews 12:15.
