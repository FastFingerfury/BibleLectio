Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about contentment?

The Bible speaks to contentment in verses such as Hebrews 13:5, “Let your conversation be without covetousness”, and 1 Timothy 6:6-8, “But godliness with contentment is great gain” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about contentment are most loved?

Among the most loved is Philippians 4:11-13, “Not that I speak in respect of want: for I have learned, in whatsoever state I am, therewith to be content.” Others are Hebrews 13:5, 1 Timothy 6:6-8 and Matthew 6:33.
