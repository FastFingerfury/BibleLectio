Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about grief?

The Bible speaks to grief in verses such as Matthew 5:4, “Blessed are they that mourn: for they shall be comforted”, and Psalms 34:18, “The LORD is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about grief are most loved?

Among the most loved is Matthew 5:4, “Blessed are they that mourn: for they shall be comforted.” Others are Psalms 34:18, 1 Thessalonians 4:13-14 and Revelation 21:4.
