Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about self-worth?

The Bible speaks to self-worth in verses such as Song of Solomon 4:7, “Thou art all fair, my love; there is no spot in thee”, and Psalms 139:13-14, “For thou hast possessed my reins: thou hast covered me in my mother’s womb” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about self-worth are most loved?

Among the most loved is Song of Solomon 4:7, “Thou art all fair, my love; there is no spot in thee.” Others are Psalms 139:13-14, Luke 12:6-7 and Matthew 10:29-31.
