Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about depression?

The Bible speaks to depression in verses such as Psalms 34:18, “The LORD is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart”, and 2 Corinthians 7:6, “Nevertheless God, that comforteth those that are cast down, comforted us by the coming of Titus” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about depression are most loved?

Among the most loved is Psalms 34:18, “The LORD is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart.” Others are 2 Corinthians 7:6, 1 Peter 5:7 and Matthew 11:28.
