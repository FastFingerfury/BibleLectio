Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about friendship?

The Bible speaks to friendship in verses such as Proverbs 27:17, “Iron sharpeneth iron; so a man sharpeneth the countenance of his friend”, and John 15:13, “Greater love hath no man than this, that a man lay down his life for his friends” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about friendship are most loved?

Among the most loved is Proverbs 27:17, “Iron sharpeneth iron; so a man sharpeneth the countenance of his friend.” Others are John 15:13, Proverbs 17:17 and Ecclesiastes 4:9-12.
