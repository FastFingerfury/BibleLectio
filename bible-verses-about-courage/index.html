Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about courage?

The Bible speaks to courage in verses such as Joshua 1:9, “Have not I commanded thee? Be strong and of a good courage”, and Deuteronomy 31:6, “Be strong and of a good courage, fear not, nor be afraid of them” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about courage are most loved?

Among the most loved is Joshua 1:9, “Have not I commanded thee? Be strong and of a good courage.” Others are Deuteronomy 31:6, 1 Corinthians 16:13 and Psalms 27:14.
