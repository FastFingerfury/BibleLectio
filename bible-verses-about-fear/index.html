Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about fear?

The Bible speaks to fear in verses such as Isaiah 41:10, “Fear thou not; for I am with thee: be not dismayed”, and Psalms 56:3-4, “What time I am afraid, I will trust in thee” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about fear are most loved?

Among the most loved is Isaiah 41:10, “Fear thou not; for I am with thee: be not dismayed.” Others are Psalms 56:3-4, Psalms 23:4 and Joshua 1:9.
