Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about giving?

The Bible speaks to giving in verses such as Proverbs 19:17, “He that hath pity upon the poor lendeth unto the LORD”, and 2 Corinthians 9:7, “Every man according as he purposeth in his heart, so let him give” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about giving are most loved?

Among the most loved is Proverbs 19:17, “He that hath pity upon the poor lendeth unto the LORD.” Others are 2 Corinthians 9:7, Luke 6:38 and Malachi 3:10.
