What does the Bible say about God's will and decisions?

The Bible speaks to God's will and decisions in verses such as Proverbs 3:5-6, “Trust in the LORD with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding”, and Jeremiah 29:11, “For I know the thoughts that I think toward you, saith the LORD, thoughts of peace, and not of evil, to give you an expected end” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about God's will and decisions are most loved?

Among the most loved is Proverbs 3:5-6, “Trust in the LORD with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding.” Others are Jeremiah 29:11, Psalms 119:105 and Proverbs 19:21.
