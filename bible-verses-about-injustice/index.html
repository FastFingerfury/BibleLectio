Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about injustice?

The Bible speaks to injustice in verses such as Micah 6:8, “He hath shewed thee, O man, what is good”, and Leviticus 19:15, “Ye shall do no unrighteousness in judgment” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about injustice are most loved?

Among the most loved is Micah 6:8, “He hath shewed thee, O man, what is good.” Others are Leviticus 19:15, Romans 12:19 and Jeremiah 22:3.
