Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about work?

The Bible speaks to work in verses such as Proverbs 16:3, “Commit thy works unto the LORD, and thy thoughts shall be established”, and Colossians 3:23-24, “And whatsoever ye do, do it heartily, as to the Lord, and not unto men” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about work are most loved?

Among the most loved is Proverbs 16:3, “Commit thy works unto the LORD, and thy thoughts shall be established.” Others are Colossians 3:23-24, Proverbs 18:9 and Proverbs 14:23.
