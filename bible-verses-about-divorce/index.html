Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about divorce?

The Bible speaks to divorce in verses such as Matthew 19:9, “And I say unto you, Whosoever shall put away his wife, except it be for fornication, and shall marry another, committeth adultery”, and Mark 10:9, “What therefore God hath joined together, let not man put asunder” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about divorce are most loved?

Among the most loved is Matthew 19:9, “And I say unto you, Whosoever shall put away his wife, except it be for fornication, and shall marry another, committeth adultery.” Others are Matthew 5:31-32, Malachi 2:14-16 and Mark 10:9.
