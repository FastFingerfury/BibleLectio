Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about comfort?

The Bible speaks to comfort in verses such as Matthew 5:4, “Blessed are they that mourn: for they shall be comforted”, and Psalms 119:50, “This is my comfort in my affliction: for thy word hath quickened me” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about comfort are most loved?

Among the most loved is 2 Corinthians 1:3-4, “Blessed be God, even the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of mercies, and the God of all comfort.” Others are Matthew 11:28-30, Matthew 5:4 and Psalms 119:50.
