Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about jealousy and envy?

The Bible speaks to jealousy and envy in verses such as Proverbs 14:30, “A sound heart is the life of the flesh: but envy the rottenness of the bones”, and Exodus 20:17, “Thou shalt not covet thy neighbour’s house, thou shalt not covet thy neighbour’s wife, nor his manservant, nor his maidservant, nor his ox, nor his ass, nor” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about jealousy and envy are most loved?

Among the most loved is Proverbs 14:30, “A sound heart is the life of the flesh: but envy the rottenness of the bones.” Others are Galatians 5:19-21, 2 Corinthians 10:12 and Exodus 20:17.
