Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about serving and gifts?

The Bible speaks to serving and gifts in verses such as 1 Peter 4:10, “As every man hath received the gift, even so minister the same one to another, as good stewards of the manifold grace of God”, and Mark 10:45, “For even the Son of man came not to be ministered unto, but to minister, and to give his life a ransom for many” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about serving and gifts are most loved?

Among the most loved is 1 Peter 4:10, “As every man hath received the gift, even so minister the same one to another, as good stewards of the manifold grace of God.” Others are Mark 10:45, Acts 20:35 and Galatians 5:13.
