Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about why bad things happen?

The Bible speaks to why bad things happen in verses such as Romans 8:28, “And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to his purpose”, and Revelation 21:4, “And God shall wipe away all tears from their eyes” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about why bad things happen are most loved?

Among the most loved is Romans 8:28, “And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to his purpose.” Others are Revelation 21:4, Genesis 50:20 and John 9:3.
