Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about suffering and trials?

The Bible speaks to suffering and trials in verses such as John 16:33, “These things I have spoken unto you, that in me ye might have peace”, and 1 Peter 5:10, “But the God of all grace, who hath called us unto his eternal glory by Christ Jesus, after that ye have suffered a while, make you perfect, stablish” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about suffering and trials are most loved?

Among the most loved is John 16:33, “These things I have spoken unto you, that in me ye might have peace.” Others are Romans 5:3-5, 1 Peter 5:10 and Romans 8:18.
