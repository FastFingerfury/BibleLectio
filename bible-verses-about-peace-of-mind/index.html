Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about peace of mind?

The Bible speaks to peace of mind in verses such as John 14:27, “Peace I leave with you, my peace I give unto you”, and Psalms 4:8, “I will both lay me down in peace, and sleep” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about peace of mind are most loved?

Among the most loved is John 14:27, “Peace I leave with you, my peace I give unto you.” Others are Philippians 4:6-7, Psalms 4:8 and Psalms 119:165.
