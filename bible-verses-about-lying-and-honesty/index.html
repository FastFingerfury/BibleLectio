Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about lying and honesty?

The Bible speaks to lying and honesty in verses such as Proverbs 12:22, “Lying lips are abomination to the LORD: but they that deal truly are his delight”, and Ephesians 4:25, “Wherefore putting away lying, speak every man truth with his neighbour” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about lying and honesty are most loved?

Among the most loved is Proverbs 12:22, “Lying lips are abomination to the LORD: but they that deal truly are his delight.” Others are Ephesians 4:25, Proverbs 6:16-19 and Colossians 3:9.
