Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about anger?

The Bible speaks to anger in verses such as James 1:19-20, “Wherefore, my beloved brethren, let every man be swift to hear, slow to speak, slow to wrath”, and Ephesians 4:26, “Be ye angry, and sin not: let not the sun go down upon your wrath” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about anger are most loved?

Among the most loved is James 1:19-20, “Wherefore, my beloved brethren, let every man be swift to hear, slow to speak, slow to wrath.” Others are Ephesians 4:26, Proverbs 15:1 and Ephesians 4:31-32.
