Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about parents and children?

The Bible speaks to parents and children in verses such as Proverbs 22:6, “Train up a child in the way he should go”, and Ephesians 6:4, “And, ye fathers, provoke not your children to wrath” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about parents and children are most loved?

Among the most loved is Proverbs 22:6, “Train up a child in the way he should go.” Others are Deuteronomy 6:6-7, Ephesians 6:1-3 and Psalms 127:3-5.
