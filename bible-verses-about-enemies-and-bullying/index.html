Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about enemies and bullying?

The Bible speaks to enemies and bullying in verses such as Matthew 5:44, “But I say unto you, Love your enemies, bless them that curse you, do good to them that hate you, and pray for them which despitefully use you, and persecute”, and Romans 12:14, “Bless them which persecute you: bless, and curse not” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about enemies and bullying are most loved?

Among the most loved is Matthew 5:44, “But I say unto you, Love your enemies, bless them that curse you, do good to them that hate you, and pray for them which despitefully use you, and persecute…” Others are Romans 12:14, Leviticus 19:18 and Matthew 5:10-12.
