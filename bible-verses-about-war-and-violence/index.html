Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about war and violence?

The Bible speaks to war and violence in verses such as Ecclesiastes 3:8, “A time to love, and a time to hate; a time of war, and a time of peace”, and Psalms 11:5, “The LORD trieth the righteous: but the wicked and him that loveth violence his soul hateth” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about war and violence are most loved?

Among the most loved is Ecclesiastes 3:8, “A time to love, and a time to hate; a time of war, and a time of peace.” Others are Isaiah 2:4, Psalms 11:5 and Exodus 14:14.
