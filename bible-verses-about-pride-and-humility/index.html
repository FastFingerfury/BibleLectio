Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about pride and humility?

The Bible speaks to pride and humility in verses such as James 4:10, “Humble yourselves in the sight of the Lord, and he shall lift you up”, and 1 Peter 5:6, “Humble yourselves therefore under the mighty hand of God, that he may exalt you in due time” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about pride and humility are most loved?

Among the most loved is James 4:10, “Humble yourselves in the sight of the Lord, and he shall lift you up.” Others are 1 Peter 5:6, Proverbs 11:2 and James 4:6.
