Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about hope?

The Bible speaks to hope in verses such as Jeremiah 29:11, “For I know the thoughts that I think toward you, saith the LORD, thoughts of peace, and not of evil, to give you an expected end”, and Isaiah 40:31, “But they that wait upon the LORD shall renew their strength” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about hope are most loved?

Among the most loved is Jeremiah 29:11, “For I know the thoughts that I think toward you, saith the LORD, thoughts of peace, and not of evil, to give you an expected end.” Others are Isaiah 40:31, Romans 12:12 and Romans 15:13.
