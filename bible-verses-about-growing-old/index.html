Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about growing old?

The Bible speaks to growing old in verses such as Isaiah 46:4, “And even to your old age I am he; and even to hoar hairs will I carry you”, and Proverbs 16:31, “The hoary head is a crown of glory, if it be found in the way of righteousness” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about growing old are most loved?

Among the most loved is Isaiah 46:4, “And even to your old age I am he; and even to hoar hairs will I carry you.” Others are Proverbs 16:31, Leviticus 19:32 and Job 12:12.
