Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about laziness?

The Bible speaks to laziness in verses such as Proverbs 18:9, “He also that is slothful in his work is brother to him that is a great waster”, and 1 Timothy 5:8, “But if any provide not for his own, and specially for those of his own house, he hath denied the faith, and is worse than an infidel” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about laziness are most loved?

Among the most loved is Proverbs 6:6-11, “Go to the ant, thou sluggard; consider her ways, and be wise.” Others are Proverbs 18:9, 1 Timothy 5:8 and Proverbs 19:15.
