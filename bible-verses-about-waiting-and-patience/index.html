Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about waiting and patience?

The Bible speaks to waiting and patience in verses such as Psalms 27:14, “Wait on the LORD: be of good courage, and he shall strengthen thine heart”, and Isaiah 40:31, “But they that wait upon the LORD shall renew their strength” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about waiting and patience are most loved?

Among the most loved is Psalms 27:14, “Wait on the LORD: be of good courage, and he shall strengthen thine heart.” Others are Isaiah 40:31, Romans 8:25 and Romans 12:12.
