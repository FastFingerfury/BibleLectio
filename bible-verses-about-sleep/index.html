Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about sleep?

The Bible speaks to sleep in verses such as Psalms 4:8, “I will both lay me down in peace, and sleep”, and Psalms 3:5, “I laid me down and slept; I awaked; for the LORD sustained me” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about sleep are most loved?

Among the most loved is Psalms 4:8, “I will both lay me down in peace, and sleep.” Others are Psalms 3:5, Psalms 127:2 and Proverbs 3:24.
