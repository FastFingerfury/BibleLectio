Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about prayer?

The Bible speaks to prayer in verses such as Colossians 4:2, “Continue in prayer, and watch in the same with thanksgiving”, and 1 Thessalonians 5:17, “Pray without ceasing” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about prayer are most loved?

Among the most loved is Colossians 4:2, “Continue in prayer, and watch in the same with thanksgiving.” Others are 1 Thessalonians 5:17, Philippians 4:6 and Matthew 7:7.
