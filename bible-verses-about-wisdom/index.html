Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about wisdom?

The Bible speaks to wisdom in verses such as James 1:5, “If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not”, and Proverbs 1:7, “The fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about wisdom are most loved?

Among the most loved is James 1:5, “If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not.” Others are Proverbs 3:13-18, Proverbs 1:7 and Proverbs 19:20.
