Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about church troubles?

The Bible speaks to church troubles in verses such as Matthew 7:15, “Beware of false prophets, which come to you in sheep’s clothing, but inwardly they are ravening wolves”, and 1 John 4:20, “If a man say, I love God, and hateth his brother, he is a liar” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about church troubles are most loved?

Among the most loved is 1 Timothy 3:1-7, “This is a true saying, If a man desire the office of a bishop, he desireth a good work.” Others are Matthew 7:15, Mark 10:42-45 and 1 John 4:20.
