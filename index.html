What makes this Bible app different.

The heart of BibleLectioThe cross dial.

Hold any verse and everything you can do with it opens under your finger: save it to your library, link it to another verse, colour its words or bookmark it. Tap a circle to look inside.

Who is speaking.

Every speaker in the King James Bible in a colour of their own, the whole way through. God and Jesus in gold, angels in blue.

Write in it.

Take notes right under any verse with an Apple Pencil or your finger, on iPad, iPhone or computer. Rather type? Tap and type.

Your library.

Save the verses that stop you into folders of your own, then pin and link them. Your library goes with you to every device.

Your words.

Keep the words you are studying, and the Bible marks them as you read. Tap one to see what it means and every verse it is in.

The map.

1,335 Bible places on a painted map of the Bible's world. Tap one to read every verse that names it.

Add verse.

Everything that goes into your library. Slide up to the brain and it opens in three. Tap one to see what it does.

Link verse.

Everything that joins verses together. Slide left to the link and it opens in three. Tap one to see what it does.

Colour.

How the words look, and passing them on. Slide right to the colour and it opens in three. Tap one to see what it does.

Bookmark verse.

Keeping your place, and marking the words. Slide down to the bookmark and it opens in three. Tap one to see what it does.

Send verse to.

Choose the folder in your library the verse goes into.

Add verse.

One tap and the verse is in your library, in the folder you have open.

Dictionary.

The word under your finger goes into your Dictionary. Hold a few words and they become a new word of yours.

Pin verse.

Pins the verse at the top of your library, with the verses joined to it. You stay where you are in the Bible.

Link verse.

Tap any other verse, in any book, and the two are joined. Tap as many as you like, then Done.

Linked.

Every verse joined to this one, listed under it with its words.

Share verse.

Turns the verse into a card, ready to send to anyone.

Colour.

Give the word a colour of your own, in the whole Bible or just in this verse.

Copy verse.

The words, where they are from and which Bible, ready to paste anywhere.

Underline.

Draws a line under the words you hold.

Bookmark verse.

Keeps your place. The verse glows so you find it again.

Many bookmarks.

A pen for bookmarks: tap verse after verse, or drag over a few to make them one.

Questions people ask.

What is BibleLectio?

BibleLectio is a Bible app for reading and studying the Bible on your phone, your iPad and your computer. Hold any verse and the cross dial opens under your finger, to save the verse to your library, link it to another verse, colour its words or bookmark it.

Which Bible app is good for studying, not only reading?

BibleLectio is made for both. Keep the verses that stop you in a library of your own, link verses to each other, write notes under them with an Apple Pencil or your finger, and tap a Hebrew word to see what it means.

Is there a Bible app that shows who is speaking?

Yes. In BibleLectio’s King James Version every speaker has a colour of their own, the whole way through the Bible. God and Jesus are in gold and angels in blue, so you always know who is talking.

Can I write notes in the Bible with an Apple Pencil?

Yes. BibleLectio opens a page under any verse that you can write on with an Apple Pencil or your finger, or type on. Your notes stay with their verse.

Which Bibles and translations does BibleLectio have?

BibleLectio has the King James Version, the World English Bible, the American Standard Version, the Reina-Valera 1909 in Spanish, the Japanese Colloquial Bible of 1955, the Hebrew Bible (Westminster Leningrad Codex) with a Hebrew New Testament (Salkinson-Ginsburg, 1886), and the Apocrypha, 1 Enoch, Jubilees, Jasher and the Testament of Moses.

Is there a Bible app with a map of Bible places?

Yes. BibleLectio puts 1,335 places from the Bible on a painted map of the Bible’s world. Tap a place to read every verse that names it.

Do I need an account to use BibleLectio?

No. Open BibleLectio in your browser and start reading. Sign in only to carry your library to every device. The iPhone and iPad app is coming to the App Store.
