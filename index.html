What makes this Bible app different.

Questions people ask.

What is BibleLectio?

BibleLectio is a Bible app for reading and studying the Bible on your phone, your iPad and your computer. Hold any verse and the cross dial opens under your finger, to save the verse to your library, link it to another verse, colour its words or bookmark it.

Which Bible app is good for studying, not only reading?

BibleLectio is made for both. Keep the verses that stop you in a library of your own, link verses to each other, write or type notes under them with an Apple Pencil or the keyboard, and tap a Hebrew word to see what it means.

Is there a Bible app that shows who is speaking?

Yes. In BibleLectio’s King James Version every speaker has a colour of their own, the whole way through the Bible. God and Jesus are in gold and angels in blue, so you always know who is talking.

Can I write notes in the Bible with an Apple Pencil?

Yes. BibleLectio opens a page under any verse that you can write on with an Apple Pencil, or type on. Your notes stay with their verse.

Which Bibles and translations does BibleLectio have?

BibleLectio has the King James Version, the World English Bible, the American Standard Version, the Reina-Valera 1909 in Spanish, the Japanese Colloquial Bible of 1955, the Hebrew Bible (Westminster Leningrad Codex) with a Hebrew New Testament (Salkinson-Ginsburg, 1886), and the Apocrypha, 1 Enoch, Jubilees, Jasher and the Testament of Moses.

Is there a Bible app with a map of Bible places?

Yes. BibleLectio puts 1,335 places from the Bible on a painted map of the Bible’s world. Tap a place to read every verse that names it.

Is there a Bible app with Strong’s numbers and a Bible dictionary?

Yes. In BibleLectio, hold a word in the King James Version and choose Dictionary on the cross dial. From then on, tap the word and its card shows what Strong’s, Easton’s Bible Dictionary and Webster’s Dictionary of 1913 say of it; tap one to read it.

How do I link Bible verses together?

In BibleLectio, hold a verse, choose Link verse on the cross dial and tap any other verse, in any book. Tap as many as you like, then Done. Linked lists every verse joined to that verse, with its words.

Is there a Bible app with reading plans that reads the Bible aloud?

Yes. BibleLectio has five reading plans, among them the Bible in a year, the New Testament in 90 days and the four Gospels in 30 days, and opens on the day’s chapter. Listen reads the chapter aloud, verse by verse, in your device’s voice.

Where can I find Bible verses about worry, fear or grief?

BibleLectio’s Topics has 69 topics for what people go through, among them worry, fear, grief, depression, anger and forgiving others. Each topic opens with the verses people love most, then every verse on it, and each has its own page at biblelectio.com/topics/.

Do I need an account to use BibleLectio?

No. Open BibleLectio in your browser and start reading. Sign in only to carry your library to every device. The iPhone and iPad app is coming to the App Store.
