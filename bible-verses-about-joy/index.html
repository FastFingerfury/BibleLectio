Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about joy?

The Bible speaks to joy in verses such as Philippians 4:4, “Rejoice in the Lord alway: and again I say, Rejoice”, and James 1:2, “My brethren, count it all joy when ye fall into divers temptations” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about joy are most loved?

Among the most loved is Philippians 4:4, “Rejoice in the Lord alway: and again I say, Rejoice.” Others are James 1:2, Nehemiah 8:10 and Romans 15:13.
