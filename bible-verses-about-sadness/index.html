Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about sadness?

The Bible speaks to sadness in verses such as Psalms 34:18, “The LORD is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart”, and Matthew 5:4, “Blessed are they that mourn: for they shall be comforted” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about sadness are most loved?

Among the most loved is Psalms 34:18, “The LORD is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart.” Others are Matthew 5:4, Psalms 147:3 and John 14:1.
