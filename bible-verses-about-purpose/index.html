Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about purpose?

The Bible speaks to purpose in verses such as Romans 8:28, “And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to his purpose”, and Jeremiah 29:11, “For I know the thoughts that I think toward you, saith the LORD, thoughts of peace, and not of evil, to give you an expected end” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about purpose are most loved?

Among the most loved is Romans 8:28, “And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to his purpose.” Others are Jeremiah 29:11, Ephesians 2:10 and Proverbs 19:21.
