What does the Bible say about doubt and feeling far from God?

The Bible speaks to doubt and feeling far from God in verses such as Mark 9:24, “And straightway the father of the child cried out, and said with tears, Lord, I believe”, and Psalms 34:18, “The LORD is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about doubt and feeling far from God are most loved?

Among the most loved is James 1:5-6, “If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not.” Others are Mark 9:24, Psalms 34:18 and Matthew 28:17.
