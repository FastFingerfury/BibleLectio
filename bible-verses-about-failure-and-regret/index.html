Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about failure and regret?

The Bible speaks to failure and regret in verses such as 1 John 1:9, “If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness”, and Proverbs 24:16, “For a just man falleth seven times, and riseth up again” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about failure and regret are most loved?

Among the most loved is Philippians 3:13-14, “Brethren, I count not myself to have apprehended.” Others are 1 John 1:9, Proverbs 24:16 and Psalms 37:23-24.
