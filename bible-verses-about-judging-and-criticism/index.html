Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about judging and criticism?

The Bible speaks to judging and criticism in verses such as John 7:24, “Judge not according to the appearance, but judge righteous judgment”, and Luke 6:37, “Judge not, and ye shall not be judged: condemn not, and ye shall not be condemned” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about judging and criticism are most loved?

Among the most loved is Matthew 7:1-5, “Judge not, that ye be not judged. For with what judgment ye judge, ye shall be judged.” Others are John 7:24, Luke 6:37 and Proverbs 27:5-6.
