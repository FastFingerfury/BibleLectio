Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about arguments and making peace?

The Bible speaks to arguments and making peace in verses such as Romans 12:18, “If it be possible, as much as lieth in you, live peaceably with all men”, and Romans 14:19, “Let us therefore follow after the things which make for peace, and things wherewith one may edify another” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about arguments and making peace are most loved?

Among the most loved is Romans 12:18, “If it be possible, as much as lieth in you, live peaceably with all men.” Others are Romans 14:19, Proverbs 15:1 and Matthew 5:9.
