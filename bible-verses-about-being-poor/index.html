Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about being poor?

The Bible speaks to being poor in verses such as Philippians 4:19, “But my God shall supply all your need according to his riches in glory by Christ Jesus”, and Hebrews 13:5, “Let your conversation be without covetousness” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about being poor are most loved?

Among the most loved is Philippians 4:19, “But my God shall supply all your need according to his riches in glory by Christ Jesus.” Others are Matthew 6:31-33, Hebrews 13:5 and Proverbs 14:31.
