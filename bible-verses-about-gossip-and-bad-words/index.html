Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about gossip and bad words?

The Bible speaks to gossip and bad words in verses such as Ephesians 4:29, “Let no corrupt communication proceed out of your mouth, but that which is good to the use of edifying, that it may minister grace unto the hearers”, and Psalms 34:13, “Keep thy tongue from evil, and thy lips from speaking guile” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about gossip and bad words are most loved?

Among the most loved is Ephesians 4:29, “Let no corrupt communication proceed out of your mouth, but that which is good to the use of edifying, that it may minister grace unto the hearers.” Others are Psalms 34:13, Proverbs 16:28 and Colossians 3:8.
