Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about self-control?

The Bible speaks to self-control in verses such as Proverbs 23:20-21, “Be not among winebibbers; among riotous eaters of flesh”, and Galatians 5:22-23, “But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, longsuffering, gentleness, goodness, faith, Meekness, temperance” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about self-control are most loved?

Among the most loved is 1 Corinthians 10:13, “There hath no temptation taken you but such as is common to man.” Others are Proverbs 23:20-21, Galatians 5:22-23 and Proverbs 23:2.
