Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about temptation?

The Bible speaks to temptation in verses such as Matthew 26:41, “Watch and pray, that ye enter not into temptation”, and Matthew 6:13, “And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about temptation are most loved?

Among the most loved is 1 Corinthians 10:13, “There hath no temptation taken you but such as is common to man.” Others are James 1:13-15, Matthew 26:41 and Matthew 6:13.
