What does the Bible say about dating and being single?

The Bible speaks to dating and being single in verses such as Genesis 2:18, “And the LORD God said, It is not good that the man should be alone”, and 2 Corinthians 6:14, “Be ye not unequally yoked together with unbelievers” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about dating and being single are most loved?

Among the most loved is Genesis 2:18, “And the LORD God said, It is not good that the man should be alone.” Others are 2 Corinthians 6:14, 1 Corinthians 15:33 and 1 Corinthians 7:7.
