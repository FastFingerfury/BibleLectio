Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about heaven and hell?

The Bible speaks to heaven and hell in verses such as 2 Corinthians 5:1, “For we know that if our earthly house of this tabernacle were dissolved, we have a building of God, an house not made with hands, eternal in the heavens”, and Hebrews 11:16, “But now they desire a better country, that is, an heavenly” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about heaven and hell are most loved?

Among the most loved is 2 Corinthians 5:1, “For we know that if our earthly house of this tabernacle were dissolved, we have a building of God, an house not made with hands, eternal in the heavens.” Others are Hebrews 11:16, Revelation 21:1-4 and John 14:2-3.
