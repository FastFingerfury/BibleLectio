Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about coming back to God?

The Bible speaks to coming back to God in verses such as Acts 3:19, “Repent ye therefore, and be converted, that your sins may be blotted out, when the times of refreshing shall come from the presence of the Lord”, and Matthew 4:17, “From that time Jesus began to preach, and to say, Repent” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about coming back to God are most loved?

Among the most loved is Acts 3:19, “Repent ye therefore, and be converted, that your sins may be blotted out, when the times of refreshing shall come from the presence of the Lord.” Others are Matthew 4:17, 2 Chronicles 7:14 and 1 John 1:9.
