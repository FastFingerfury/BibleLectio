Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about love?

The Bible speaks to love in verses such as John 13:34-35, “A new commandment I give unto you, That ye love one another”, and Leviticus 19:18, “Thou shalt not avenge, nor bear any grudge against the children of thy people, but thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about love are most loved?

Among the most loved is 1 Corinthians 13:4-7, “Charity suffereth long, and is kind; charity envieth not.” Others are John 13:34-35, Leviticus 19:18 and Matthew 22:37-39.
