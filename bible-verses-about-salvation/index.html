Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about salvation?

The Bible speaks to salvation in verses such as Ephesians 2:8-9, “For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves”, and Romans 6:23, “For the wages of sin is death; but the gift of God is eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about salvation are most loved?

Among the most loved is Ephesians 2:8-9, “For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves.” Others are Romans 6:23, Romans 10:9 and John 3:16.
