Jordan in the Bible.

Questions about Jordan.

Where is Jordan in the Bible?

Jordan is a river about 31 km (19 miles) east of Jerusalem. BibleLectio shows it on a painted map of the Bible’s world, with every verse that names it.

How many times is Jordan named in the Bible?

The King James Version names Jordan in 177 verses, in 21 books, from Genesis 32:10 to John 10:40.

Which people are named with Jordan in the Bible?

The verses that name Jordan also name Moses, Joshua, Reuben, Manasseh, Gad and David, among others.
