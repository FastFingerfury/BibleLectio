Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about addiction and drinking?

The Bible speaks to addiction and drinking in verses such as Proverbs 20:1, “Wine is a mocker, strong drink is raging”, and John 8:36, “If the Son therefore shall make you free, ye shall be free indeed” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about addiction and drinking are most loved?

Among the most loved is Proverbs 20:1, “Wine is a mocker, strong drink is raging.” Others are John 8:36, 1 Corinthians 6:12 and Galatians 5:21.
