Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about sickness and healing?

The Bible speaks to sickness and healing in verses such as Jeremiah 17:14, “Heal me, O LORD, and I shall be healed; save me, and I shall be saved”, and Psalms 147:3, “He healeth the broken in heart, and bindeth up their wounds” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about sickness and healing are most loved?

Among the most loved is Jeremiah 17:14, “Heal me, O LORD, and I shall be healed; save me, and I shall be saved.” Others are Psalms 147:3, James 5:14-15 and Isaiah 53:5.
