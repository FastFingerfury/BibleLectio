Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about marriage?

The Bible speaks to marriage in verses such as Mark 10:9, “What therefore God hath joined together, let not man put asunder”, and Genesis 2:24, “Therefore shall a man leave his father and his mother, and shall cleave unto his wife” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about marriage are most loved?

Among the most loved is Mark 10:9, “What therefore God hath joined together, let not man put asunder.” Others are Ephesians 5:25-33, Ecclesiastes 4:9-12 and Genesis 2:24.
