Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about anxiety and worry?

The Bible speaks to anxiety and worry in verses such as 1 Peter 5:7, “Casting all your care upon him; for he careth for you”, and John 14:27, “Peace I leave with you, my peace I give unto you” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about anxiety and worry are most loved?

Among the most loved is 1 Peter 5:7, “Casting all your care upon him; for he careth for you.” Others are Matthew 6:25-34, Philippians 4:6-7 and John 14:27.
