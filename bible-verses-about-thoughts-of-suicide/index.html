Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about thoughts of suicide?

The Bible speaks to thoughts of suicide in verses such as Psalms 34:18, “The LORD is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart”, and Deuteronomy 30:19, “I call heaven and earth to record this day against you, that I have set before you life and death, blessing and cursing” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about thoughts of suicide are most loved?

Among the most loved is Psalms 34:18, “The LORD is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart.” Others are Deuteronomy 30:19, Psalms 147:3 and Isaiah 41:10.
