Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about new beginnings?

The Bible speaks to new beginnings in verses such as 2 Corinthians 5:17, “Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature”, and Lamentations 3:22-23, “It is of the LORD’S mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about new beginnings are most loved?

Among the most loved is 2 Corinthians 5:17, “Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature.” Others are Isaiah 43:18-19, Lamentations 3:22-23 and Jeremiah 29:11.
