Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about abuse?

The Bible speaks to abuse in verses such as Psalms 34:18, “The LORD is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart”, and Psalms 147:3, “He healeth the broken in heart, and bindeth up their wounds” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about abuse are most loved?

Among the most loved is Psalms 34:18, “The LORD is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart.” Others are Psalms 147:3, Psalms 9:9 and Colossians 3:19.
