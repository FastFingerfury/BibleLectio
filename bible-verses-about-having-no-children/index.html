Questions people ask.

What does the Bible say about having no children?

The Bible speaks to having no children in verses such as Psalms 113:9, “He maketh the barren woman to keep house, and to be a joyful mother of children”, and 1 Samuel 1:27, “For this child I prayed; and the LORD hath given me my petition which I asked of him” (King James Version).

Which Bible verses about having no children are most loved?

Among the most loved is Psalms 113:9, “He maketh the barren woman to keep house, and to be a joyful mother of children.” Others are 1 Samuel 1:27, Hebrews 11:11 and Genesis 18:14.
